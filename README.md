## 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a **Mechanical and Production Engineering** undergraduate at **Ahsanullah University of Science and Technology (AUST)**, with experience and interests spanning engineering, research, entrepreneurship, business operations, and creative work.

My technical exposure includes **CAD modelling**, **structural analysis**, **renewable-energy simulation**, **Arduino-based instrumentation**, and **bio-composite additive manufacturing**. Alongside my academic work, I have contributed to business development, customer relationship management, service delivery, team coordination, and student-led ventures.

I enjoy connecting technical problem-solving with practical execution. Whether I am exploring an engineering concept, coordinating a project, developing a business initiative, or creating visual content, my goal is to turn ideas into useful outcomes.

> **Core Focus Areas:** `Mechanical Engineering` · `Production & Manufacturing` · `Product Development` · `Academic Research` · `Renewable Energy` · `Additive Manufacturing` · `Entrepreneurship`
